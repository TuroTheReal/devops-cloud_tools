# Avoir (credit note / corrective invoice): [no.]

> ⚠️ These templates follow **French law** (you invoice from France); only the language is English. French legal mentions are kept verbatim.
> **Why this doc:** to **correct/cancel an invoice after it has been issued** (an invoice is never modified). ≠ avenant, which modifies the contract before invoicing.
> Document that **cancels or corrects an already-issued invoice** (an invoice is neither modified nor deleted).
> Same form as an invoice, with a **negative amount** and a **reference to the original invoice**.
> ⚠️ It is **not** an avenant (amendment): the avenant modifies the contract **before** invoicing; the avoir corrects an invoice **after** it is issued. Not legal advice — see `SOURCES.md`.
> Replace the `[placeholders]`.
