# CGV (French general terms of sale)

> ⚠️ These templates follow **French law** (you invoice from France); only the language is English. French legal mentions are kept verbatim.
> **Why this doc:** the contractual baseline shared by every mission. The quote carries the scope and the price, these terms carry the rules. The quote references them (§10 of the quote), and accepting the quote makes them binding.
> **Structure:** one document for every offer, one version to cite. Part 1 applies to every mission. Part 2 holds one chapter per offer; only the chapter of the offer named on the quote applies.
> **How to use it:** a PDF attached to every quote, never a bare link. In B2B, article L.441-1 of the French commercial code makes them the basis of commercial negotiation: they must be provided to any professional buyer who asks for them.
> **Version them.** The quote must cite the accepted version, otherwise you cannot prove which one applied. Version and date also go in `content-ops/freelance/provider.yml` (`cgv_version`, `cgv_date`), read by `/site-brief` and `/ai-brief`.
> **In case of discrepancy between this English version and the French one, the French version prevails.**
> ⚠️ Not legal advice. Have a lawyer review this once before your first mission (content-ops#21). Points marked **[to check]** rely on rules that change or depend on your status.
> Replace the `[placeholders]`. Delete this block before sending.

**Version 1.0 dated 26/11/2026**, applicable to any quote issued from this date.

# Part 1: terms common to every mission

## 1. Purpose and scope

These general terms govern any service performed by the Provider for a professional client, hereinafter "the Client".

**Provider**

- [name], entrepreneur individuel (EI, French sole proprietorship)
- [full address]
- SIRET [no.], activities: computer programming, DevOps and Cloud engineering, software development, consulting, creation, redesign and maintenance of websites, configuration of digital tools and artificial intelligence assistants, audit, go-live, supervision and maintenance of IT infrastructure, technical writing
- [email]

Any notice provided for herein, in particular the formal notices of §11, §22 and §23, is validly sent to this address.

They apply to the exclusion of any other document, in particular the Client's general terms of purchase, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

The Client acknowledges having read and accepted them before signing the quote.

## 2. Contractual documents

The contract consists of the following documents, in decreasing order of priority:

1. signed amendments (avenants), from the most recent to the oldest;
2. the quote signed "bon pour accord";
3. these general terms, part 2 (chapter of the offer named on the quote), then part 1;
4. the personal data processing agreement attached to the quote and, where applicable, the non-disclosure agreement.

In case of contradiction, the higher-ranking document prevails.

## 3. Formation of the contract

The quote is valid for **[30] days** from its issue. The contract is formed on the date the Provider receives the quote signed with the mention "bon pour accord", **and the deposit is cashed**.

No service starts before both conditions are met.

## 4. Scope

The included scope, the deliverables and the acceptance criteria are set out in the quote. Anything not written there is out of scope.

The following are excluded from any mission, **unless the quote expressly includes them**:

- application development;
- on-call duty, standby and continuous supervision;
- any commitment on the availability or performance of the Client's service;
- monitoring of the service after delivery;
- data migration outside the scope described in the quote;
- design, content writing and search engine optimisation.

The Provider promises no ranking in search engines or in the answers of artificial intelligence assistants.

## 5. Provider's obligations

The Provider is bound by an **obligation of means** (best efforts), not of result. It performs the service according to the state of the art and the knowledge available at the time of performance.

Where the service or its deliverables rely on an artificial intelligence service, the Client acknowledges that the results produced by this service are not deterministic: they may vary from one run to another and change with its publisher's updates. Conformity of the deliverables is assessed at acceptance, against the acceptance criteria of the quote. The results produced by this service after acceptance are proposals, which the Client checks before any use and for which it remains responsible.

It informs the Client without delay of any event likely to affect the scope, the timeline or the price.

## 6. Client's obligations

The Client undertakes to:

- appoint a single contact person with decision-making authority;
- provide in due time the access, environments, information and documents required;
- **have backups of its data and systems, and have verified their restoration**, before any intervention;
- answer validation requests within the time set in the quote;
- declare, before signing the quote, the data and regulations referred to in §16 bis.

Any delay attributable to the Client shifts the schedule accordingly and may give rise to an amendment.

## 7. Change of scope

Any change to the scope, deliverables, timeline or price is subject to an **amendment signed** by both parties (article 1193 of the French civil code).

No out-of-scope service is performed or invoiced without an amendment.

## 8. Timelines

The timelines in the quote run from the formation of the contract, provided the Client has met the prerequisites of §6.

They are indicative, unless expressly described as firm in the quote.

## 8 bis. Suspension caused by the Client

If the Client fails to provide the required access, information or approvals for **[15] consecutive working days**, the mission is deemed suspended by the Client.

The Provider may then invoice the services performed up to the suspension date, and is no longer bound by the initial schedule. Resumption is set by a new milestone agreed in writing, subject to its availability.

Beyond **[60] days** of suspension, the Provider may end the mission; the services performed remain due and the deposit remains acquired by the Provider.

## 9. Acceptance

At the end of the service, the Provider delivers the deliverables to the Client, who has **[8] working days** to check them against the acceptance criteria of the quote. The chapter of the offer (part 2) specifies what is delivered and checked.

The Client signs an acceptance report, with or without reservations. **Failing written and reasoned reservations within this period, acceptance is deemed given without reservations.**

Acceptance without reservations closes the mission, makes the balance payable where the chapter of the offer so provides, and starts the warranty.

## 10. Warranty

The Provider corrects free of charge, for **[30] days** from acceptance, any non-conformity of the deliverables with the acceptance criteria of the quote.

The warranty covers the configuration and deliverables as working on the date of acceptance. Changes to third-party services after delivery (features, connectors, prices, terms of use, in particular of artificial intelligence publishers, hosting providers and connected tools) are not covered; taking them into account is subject to a quote.

Excluded from the warranty:

- changes and new requests, which require an amendment;
- malfunctions resulting from a change made by the Client or a third party;
- failures **and changes** of third-party services, software or hardware;
- variation of the results of an artificial intelligence service after acceptance, provided the acceptance criteria of the quote were met;
- day-to-day operations, which fall to the Client after handover.

Warranty work takes place during business hours.

## 11. Price, invoicing and payment

Prices are in euros, excluding taxes.

**TVA non applicable, article 293 B du code général des impôts** (VAT not applicable, article 293 B of the French Tax Code).

- **Deposit and balance**: according to the schedule of the offer's chapter (part 2), unless a different schedule is written in the quote.
- **Payment term**: [15] days from the invoice date.

In case of late payment, the following are due automatically and without formal notice:

- **late-payment interest** at the rate applied by the European Central Bank to its most recent refinancing operation, plus 10 percentage points;
- a **fixed recovery fee of €40** (article L.441-10 of the French commercial code), without prejudice to further compensation on proof.

**No discount is granted for early payment.**

The Provider may suspend performance of the service, after a formal notice left unanswered for [8] days, in case of non-payment of an instalment.

## 12. Intellectual property

### 12.1 Assignment

Subject to **payment in full** of the price, the Provider assigns to the Client, exclusively, the economic rights in the deliverables specifically produced for the mission.

In accordance with article L.131-3 of the French intellectual property code, the assignment covers:

- **the rights assigned**: reproduction, representation, adaptation, modification, translation and making available of the deliverables, on any medium;
- **the purpose**: the internal and external needs of the Client's business, including maintenance and evolution by the Client or a third party of its choice;
- **the territory**: worldwide;
- **the duration**: the legal term of copyright protection.

Until payment in full, the Client has a mere right of use, which is revocable.

### 12.2 Reusable elements

The Provider retains full ownership of its **tools, scripts, modules, templates and know-how, whether pre-existing or developed independently of the mission**.

Where a deliverable incorporates them, the Provider grants the Client a **non-exclusive, irrevocable, worldwide licence, for the term of protection of the rights, transferable to the Client's successors**, to use, modify and develop the deliverable.

This reservation in no way restricts the Client's use of the deliverable.

### 12.3 Third-party and open source components

The deliverables may incorporate third-party components, in particular open source ones, subject to their own licences. The Provider provides the list on request.

These components are neither assigned nor warranted by the Provider, who is not their author. The Client is responsible for complying with their licences in its own use.

### 12.4 Content supplied by the Client

The Client warrants that it holds the rights to the texts, images, logos, trademarks and other content it supplies or asks to be reused. It holds the Provider harmless against any third-party claim relating to such content.

## 13. Liability

The Provider's liability may only be incurred in case of proven fault.

**It is capped, for all damages combined, at the amount excluding taxes actually paid by the Client for the mission concerned.**

Indirect damages are excluded, in particular: operating loss, loss of revenue, loss of customers, damage to reputation, and **loss of data where the Client did not have verified backups** in accordance with §6.

The Provider is not liable for the consequences of an interruption, failure or change of third-party services, in particular hosting providers, cloud providers, internet access, network or energy providers, and artificial intelligence publishers, nor for the consequences of a natural event, unless they result from a fault of the Provider.

No penalty, service credit or liquidated damages are owed by the Provider, unless expressly stipulated in the quote.

These limitations apply neither in case of gross negligence or wilful misconduct, nor where the law excludes them.

## 14. Insurance

The Provider declares that it holds professional liability insurance covering its activity. It provides the certificate on request.

## 15. Confidentiality

Each party undertakes not to disclose the other's confidential information, during the mission and for **[2] years** after its end.

The Provider undertakes in particular not to disclose any information relating to the architecture, volumes, data or vulnerabilities of the Client's system.

The following are not confidential:

- public information, information already known, or information whose disclosure is required by law;
- **the Provider's methods, tools, scripts and know-how**, including when used during the mission;
- **the very fact of the mission and its non-technical description**, whose communication remains governed by §19.

This clause does not prevent the application of §19.

## 16. Personal data

Where the mission leads the Provider to process personal data on behalf of the Client, the Client acts as controller and the Provider as processor within the meaning of article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.

A **data processing agreement (article 28 GDPR)** is attached to every quote; signing the quote accepts this agreement. Failing this, the Provider accesses no personal data.

The Provider informs the Client without delay of any personal data breach it becomes aware of, so that the Client can meet its own notification obligations.

The Provider keeps no Client data beyond the mission, except redacted intervention logs.

## 16 bis. Sensitive data and sector regulations

The Client declares in writing, **before signing the quote**, whether the mission involves or gives access to:

- health data or other special categories of data within the meaning of article 9 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679;
- information covered by professional secrecy (in particular medical or legal);
- data of minors;
- a system subject to sector regulation (in particular security of network and information systems, digital operational resilience of the financial sector, payment card data).

Failing a declaration, the mission is deemed to involve none. The Provider may decline all or part of a mission in view of this declaration.

In any event:

- the Provider **does not host health data** and **does not administer or operate any information system containing it**, as it is not a certified health data host (hébergeur de données de santé). It may design or deploy an architecture on the Client's certified host, without access to health data, with that host's written agreement;
- the Provider does not host data whose regulation requires qualified or certified hosting;
- the Provider neither stores nor processes payment card data; payments go through a certified payment service provider chosen by the Client;
- no health data and no information covered by professional secrecy is sent to an artificial intelligence service without the Client's prior written agreement, after checking that the service allows it.

The Client remains responsible for its compliance with the regulations applicable to its business.

## 16 ter. Data entrusted to the Client's third-party services

The third-party services used in the mission (artificial intelligence publisher, hosting provider, mailbox, calendar, notes tool, other connected tools) are subscribed by the Client, in its name and at its expense, and are governed by its contracts with their publishers.

The Client remains responsible for the data it entrusts to them and for the use their publishers make of it, in particular the use of conversations to train their models: the Provider informs the Client of this setting, and the Client decides.

## 17. Security and access

- Access is **personal** and created by the Client. The Provider never holds the main administration credentials.
- Secrets go through a secrets manager, never by messaging or in a code repository.
- **No change is made without a verified backup and a tested restore.**
- Interventions take place on a test environment where one exists; otherwise, in a window agreed in writing, with a rollback plan.
- An intervention log is kept and handed to the Client.
- At the end of the mission, unless a follow-up contract is in force, the Client **revokes the Provider's access and confirms the revocation in writing**.

## 18. Handover and transfer of responsibility

The service ends with a handover: delivery of the documentation specific to the offer (runbook, handover guide or getting-started document, depending on the offer's chapter) and a handover session.

From acceptance, **the operation, monitoring and maintenance of the systems and tools delivered fall to the Client**, unless a separate follow-up contract exists.

## 19. Commercial references

Unless the Client refuses in writing, the Provider may cite the Client's name and logo and a non-confidential description of the mission, as a reference, in its communication materials.

Any detailed case study is subject to the Client's written approval before publication.

A discount granted in the quote may have this authorisation as its counterpart. **It may in no case be conditional on the publication of a favourable review.**

## 20. Independence of the parties

The Provider works fully independently. It freely organises its work, hours and means, and is under no subordination.

These terms create neither a company, nor a mandate, nor an employment contract between the parties.

## 21. Subcontracting

The Provider may entrust all or part of the service to a subcontractor, under its full responsibility, after **prior written notice** to the Client.

## 22. Force majeure

Neither party is liable for a failure resulting from force majeure within the meaning of article 1218 of the French civil code.

If the event lasts more than **[30] days**, either party may terminate by registered letter, the services performed remaining due.

## 23. Termination

In case of serious breach by one party, the other may terminate after a formal notice left unanswered for **[15] days**.

The services performed and the costs incurred are then due. The deposit remains acquired by the Provider when the termination is attributable to the Client.

## 24. Miscellaneous

If a clause is declared void, the others remain applicable.

Failure to rely on a clause does not constitute a waiver.

## 25. Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by **French law**.

The parties shall endeavour to settle any dispute amicably. Failing agreement within [30] days, the dispute is brought before the competent French courts.

# Part 2: terms specific to each offer

Only the chapter of the offer named on the quote applies. It supplements part 1 and prevails over it in case of contradiction. The chapters of the other offers have no effect and create no right for the Client.

## A. Infrastructure missions

*This chapter only applies if the quote is for an infrastructure mission.*

Audit, go-live, monitoring, deployment automation, production migration, infrastructure as code.

**A.1 Schedule.** Deposit of [40]% on order; balance on acceptance without reservations.

**A.2 Production interventions.** Before any intervention on a production environment: verified backup, tested restore and rollback plan ready (§17). The steps of a migration are reversible one by one.

**A.3 Audit.** The audit is read-only: no change to the Client's system. The deliverable is a written report.

**A.4 After handover.** The runbook and documentation are delivered at handover. Operation, monitoring and on-call duty fall to the Client (§18).

## B. Website redesign

*This chapter only applies if the quote is for a website redesign.*

**B.1 Base scope.** New interface on the Client's existing texts and photos, mobile version included; existing functions reproduced as they are; existing addresses (URLs) kept; technical search engine optimisation; legal notice page kept, or created from the Client's information; go-live with a reversible switch of the domain name, without changing the Client's mailbox unless agreed in writing. The options sold are listed in the quote.

**B.2 Content.** Texts and photos are reused as they exist or as the Client supplies them. Writing, translating or creating them falls under an option or a quote. §12.4 applies.

**B.3 Revisions.** [2] rounds of revisions are included. Each further round is invoiced as per the quote.

**B.4 Accounts and costs.** The domain name, the hosting and, where applicable, the code repository are subscribed in the Client's name, on its accounts and at its expense (§3 ter of the quote). The Provider is added as a member for the duration of the mission. During creation, a non-indexed preview may be hosted on the Provider's account; it is deleted after go-live.

**B.5 Schedule.** Deposit of [40]% on order; balance on approval of the preview, **before go-live**.

**B.6 Acceptance.** Acceptance (§9) covers the preview. Go-live takes place after payment of the balance.

**B.7 Delivery of the code.** With the site administration option (back-office), the code repository is transferred to the Client's account. Without this option, delivery includes deployment on the Client's hosting account and a complete archive of the source code.

**B.8 After delivery.** Content changes and new features are subject to a quote. The warranty of §10 covers only defects in the deliverables.

## C. Setting up an artificial intelligence environment

*This chapter only applies if the quote is for setting up an artificial intelligence environment.*

**C.1 Scope.** Configuration, in the Client's account, of its artificial intelligence assistant: preferences, context document, instructions, connectors authorised by the Client, and the tasks listed in the quote, each with its size (simple or advanced) and its trigger (on demand or scheduled). The installation call is a handover of what was installed; it is not a training action.

**C.2 Prerequisites.** Before the installation call, the Client has created in its name and at its expense the account and the subscription of the artificial intelligence service, decided on the model training setting (§16 ter) and prepared its notes tool. The personal data processing agreement (§16), attached to the quote, is accepted before any access to a mailbox.

**C.3 Tests.** The tasks are built and tested on fictitious data. Without a test on the Client's real tools and data, and because the results of an artificial intelligence vary, the Provider cannot guarantee that a task is perfect from the installation: adjustments are to be expected. They are made during the installation call and at the adjustment point.

**C.4 Schedule.** Deposit of [40]% on signature; balance after the demonstration, **before the installation call**.

**C.5 Acceptance.** Each task gives the expected result on the test set shown at the demonstration, then works on the Client's real tools during the installation call. Anything that does not work on the Client's real tools at handover is corrected free of charge.

**C.6 Adjustment point.** One one-hour session, at the Client's request, within [30] days of the installation, to tune what was delivered (frequency, tone, format). It reopens neither the scope nor acceptance, and lapses if not requested within that period.

**C.7 Quality of results.** The quality of the results depends on the Client keeping the context up to date.

<details>
<summary><b>How far you can lean in your favour</b> <i>(internal note, delete before sending)</i></summary>

Terms imposed without negotiation form a **contract of adhesion** (contrat d'adhésion). Article 1171 of the French civil code deems **unwritten** any clause creating a significant imbalance, and article L.442-1 of the French commercial code makes whoever imposes it between professionals liable.

Practical consequence: leaning too far does not protect you more, it makes **the whole clause fall** and you end up with nothing. The liability cap is the best example: capped at the mission amount it holds, excluded entirely it falls.

Already leaning as far as the law allows: obligation of means (§5), backups on the Client (§6), deemed acceptance (§9), liability cap (§13), reservation of reusable tools (§12.2).

Where not to go: excluding all liability, removing the warranty, requiring a 100% deposit, forbidding any reservation at acceptance. Each would backfire.

**Aligned with the professional liability insurance (Hiscox TECH0623, content-ops `rc-pro.md`)**: §13 excludes what the policy excludes (third-party provider outages, natural events, penalties and service credits). Never promise in a quote more than the insurance covers.

**Small businesses [to check]**: a contract concluded off-premises (in physical presence, outside your premises) with a professional employing 5 people or fewer, for a service outside its main activity, gives it in particular 14 days to withdraw (article L.221-3 of the French consumer code, DGCCRF fact sheet). Signing remotely (email, electronic signature) avoids the case.
