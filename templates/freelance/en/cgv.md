# CGV (French general terms of sale)

> ⚠️ These templates follow **French law** (you invoice from France); only the language is English. French legal mentions are kept verbatim.
> **Why this doc:** the contractual baseline shared by every mission. The quote carries the scope and the price, these terms carry the rules. The quote references them (§10), and accepting the quote makes them binding.
> **How to use it:** a PDF attached to every quote, never a bare link. In B2B, article L.441-1 of the French commercial code makes them the basis of commercial negotiation: they must be provided to any professional buyer who asks for them.
> **Version them.** The quote must cite the accepted version, otherwise you cannot prove which one applied.
> **In case of discrepancy between this English version and the French one, the French version prevails.**
> ⚠️ Not legal advice. Have a lawyer review this once before your first mission. Points marked **[to check]** rely on rules that change or depend on your status.
> Replace the `[placeholders]`. Delete this block before sending.

## 1. Purpose and scope

These general terms govern any service performed by **[name], entrepreneur individuel (EI, French sole trader)**, SIRET [no.], hereinafter "the Provider", for a professional client, hereinafter "the Client".

They apply to the exclusion of any other document, in particular the Client's general purchasing terms, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

The Client acknowledges having read and accepted them before signing the quote.

## 2. Contract documents

The contract consists of the following documents, in decreasing order of precedence:

1. signed amendments, most recent first;
2. the quote signed "bon pour accord";
3. these general terms;
4. where applicable, the non-disclosure agreement and the personal data processing agreement.

In case of contradiction, the higher-ranking document prevails.

## 3. Formation of the contract

The quote is valid for **[30] days** from issue. The contract is formed when the Provider receives the quote signed with the handwritten words "bon pour accord", **and the deposit has cleared**.

No work starts before both conditions are met.

## 4. Scope

The included scope, the deliverables and the acceptance criteria are set out in the quote. Anything not written there is out of scope.

The following are excluded from every mission unless stated otherwise in the quote:

- application development;
- on-call duty, standby and continuous monitoring;
- any availability or performance commitment on the Client's service;
- watching over the service after delivery;
- data migration beyond the scope described in the quote;
- design, content writing and search engine optimisation.

## 5. Provider's obligations

The Provider has an **obligation de moyens** (best-efforts obligation), not an obligation of result. Work is performed according to professional standards and the state of knowledge at the time of performance.

The Provider informs the Client without delay of any event likely to affect the scope, the timeline or the price.

## 6. Client's obligations

The Client undertakes to:

- appoint a single point of contact with decision-making authority;
- provide in good time the access, environments, information and documents required;
- **hold backups of its data and systems, and have tested their restoration**, before any intervention;
- respond to validation requests within the period set out in the quote.

Any delay attributable to the Client shifts the timeline accordingly and may give rise to an amendment.

## 7. Change of scope

Any change to the scope, the deliverables, the timeline or the price is subject to a **signed amendment** (article 1193 of the French civil code).

No out-of-scope work is performed or invoiced without an amendment.

## 8. Timelines

The timelines set out in the quote run from formation of the contract, provided the Client has met the prerequisites in §6.

They are indicative unless expressly stated as firm in the quote.

## 8 bis. Suspension caused by the Client

If the Client fails to provide the required access, information or validations for **[15] consecutive business days**, the mission is deemed suspended by the Client.

The Provider may then invoice the work performed as at the suspension date, and is no longer bound by the original timeline. Resumption is subject to a new milestone agreed in writing, subject to the Provider's availability.

Beyond **[60] days** of suspension, the Provider may terminate the mission; work performed remains payable and the deposit remains acquired by the Provider.

## 9. Acceptance

On completion, the Provider hands over the deliverables and the Client has **[8] business days** to check them against the acceptance criteria in the quote.

The Client signs an acceptance report, with or without reservations. **Failing a written and reasoned reservation within that period, acceptance is deemed granted without reservation.**

Acceptance without reservation closes the mission, makes the balance payable and starts the warranty.

## 10. Warranty

For **[30] days** from acceptance, the Provider corrects free of charge any non-conformity of the deliverables with the acceptance criteria in the quote.

Excluded from the warranty:

- enhancements and new requests, which require an amendment;
- malfunctions resulting from a change made by the Client or a third party;
- failures **and changes** in third-party services, software or hardware;
- day-to-day operation, which is the Client's responsibility after handover.

Warranty work is carried out during business hours.

## 11. Price, invoicing and payment

Prices are in euros, excluding tax.

**TVA non applicable, article 293 B du code général des impôts** (VAT not applicable, French small-business exemption). **[to check]** — replaced by "TVA non applicable, article L.223-3 du CIBS" since 1 September 2026, both wordings accepted until the end of 2027.

- **Deposit**: [30] % of the total, on order.
- **Balance**: on acceptance, invoiced against the acceptance report.
- **Payment term**: [30] days from the invoice date.

In case of late payment, the following are due automatically and without formal notice:

- **late payment interest** at the rate applied by the European Central Bank to its most recent refinancing operation, plus 10 percentage points;
- a **fixed recovery fee of 40 €** (article L.441-10 of the French commercial code), without prejudice to further compensation on proof.

**No discount is granted for early payment.**

The Provider may suspend performance, after a formal notice left unanswered for [8] days, if an instalment is unpaid.

## 12. Intellectual property

### 12.1 Assignment

Subject to **payment in full**, the Provider assigns to the Client, on an exclusive basis, the economic rights in the deliverables specifically produced for the mission.

In accordance with article L.131-3 of the French intellectual property code, the assignment covers:

- **rights assigned**: reproduction, representation, adaptation, modification, translation and making available of the deliverables, on any medium;
- **purpose**: the internal and external operating needs of the Client's business, including maintenance and further development by the Client or a third party of its choosing;
- **territory**: worldwide;
- **duration**: the legal term of copyright protection.

Until payment in full, the Client holds a mere right of use, revocable.

### 12.2 Reserved reusable components

The Provider retains full ownership of its **tools, scripts, modules, templates and know-how pre-existing or developed independently of the mission**.

Where a deliverable incorporates any of these, the Provider grants the Client a **non-exclusive, irrevocable, worldwide licence, for the term of protection, transferable to the Client's successors**, to use, modify and further develop the deliverable.

This reservation in no way restricts the Client's use of the deliverable.

### 12.3 Third-party and open source components

Deliverables may incorporate third-party components, in particular open source, subject to their own licences. The Provider supplies the list on request.

These components are neither assigned nor warranted by the Provider, who is not their author. The Client is responsible for complying with their licences in its own use.

## 13. Liability

The Provider is liable only in case of proven fault.

**Liability is capped, all damages combined, at the amount excluding tax actually paid by the Client for the mission concerned.**

Indirect damages are excluded, in particular: loss of operation, loss of revenue, loss of customers, damage to image, and **loss of data where the Client did not hold verified backups** as required by §6.

These limitations do not apply in case of gross negligence or wilful misconduct, nor where the law sets them aside.

## 14. Insurance

The Provider declares holding professional indemnity insurance covering its activity, and supplies the certificate on request.

## 15. Confidentiality

Each party undertakes not to disclose the other's confidential information, during the mission and for **[2] years** afterwards.

The Provider undertakes in particular not to disclose any information about the architecture, volumes, data or vulnerabilities of the Client's systems.

The following are not confidential:

- information that is public, already known, or whose disclosure is required by law;
- **the Provider's methods, tools, scripts and know-how**, including where they were used during the mission;
- **the existence of the mission and its non-technical description**, whose communication remains governed by §19.

This clause does not stand in the way of §19.

## 16. Personal data

Where the mission leads the Provider to process personal data on the Client's behalf, the Client acts as controller and the Provider as processor within the meaning of article 28 of regulation (EU) 2016/679.

A **separate data processing agreement** is then signed before any access. Failing that, the Provider accesses no personal data.

The Provider retains no Client data beyond the mission, other than redacted intervention logs.

## 17. Security and access

- Access is **named and individual**, created by the Client. The Provider never holds the Client's root or owner credentials.
- Secrets travel through a secrets manager, never through messaging or a code repository.
- **No change is made without a verified backup and a tested restore.**
- Work is carried out on a staging environment where one exists; failing that, within a window agreed in writing, with a rollback plan.
- An intervention log is kept and handed to the Client.
- At the end of the mission, the Client **revokes the Provider's access and confirms the revocation in writing**.

## 18. Handover and transfer of responsibility

The service ends with the delivery of documentation, a runbook and a handover session.

From acceptance onwards, **operation, monitoring and maintenance of the systems are the Client's responsibility**.

## 19. Commercial references

Unless the Client refuses in writing, the Provider may cite the Client's name and logo and a non-confidential description of the mission as a reference in its communication material.

Any detailed case study is subject to the Client's written approval before publication.

A discount granted in the quote may be given in exchange for this authorisation. **It may never be conditional on the publication of a favourable review.**

## 20. Independence of the parties

The Provider works in full independence, freely organising its work, hours and means, with no relationship of subordination.

These terms create no partnership, agency or employment relationship between the parties.

## 21. Subcontracting

The Provider may entrust all or part of the service to a subcontractor, under its own full responsibility, after **prior written notice** to the Client.

## 22. Force majeure

Neither party is liable for a failure resulting from force majeure within the meaning of article 1218 of the French civil code.

If the event lasts more than **[30] days**, either party may terminate by registered letter, work already performed remaining payable.

## 23. Termination

In case of serious breach by one party, the other may terminate after a formal notice left unanswered for **[15] days**.

Work performed and costs incurred are then payable. The deposit remains acquired by the Provider where termination is attributable to the Client.

## 24. Miscellaneous

If a clause is held void, the others remain applicable.

Failure to rely on a clause is not a waiver of it.

## 25. Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by **French law**.

The parties will seek an amicable settlement of any dispute. Failing agreement within [30] days, the dispute is brought before the competent French courts.

An intellectual service provided as an entrepreneur individuel does not necessarily fall into that category: have a lawyer decide before naming a specific court.

<details>
<summary><b>How far you can tilt it your way</b> <i>(internal note, delete before sending)</i></summary>

Standard terms imposed without negotiation form a **contrat d'adhésion**. Article 1171 of the French civil code deems **unwritten** any clause creating a significant imbalance, and article L.442-1 of the commercial code makes the party imposing it liable, between professionals.

Practical consequence: tilting further does not protect you more, it makes the **whole clause fall away** and you are left with nothing. The liability cap is the clearest example: capped at the mission amount it holds, excluded entirely it collapses.

Already tilted as far as the law allows: best-efforts obligation (§5), backups on the Client (§6), tacit acceptance (§9), liability cap (§13), reserved reusable tooling (§12.2).

Where not to go: excluding all liability, removing the warranty, requiring a 100 % deposit, forbidding any reservation at acceptance. Each of those would backfire.

</details>
